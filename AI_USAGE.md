2026-10-04 · "Add ADR-5: no login for now, and why"	Claude (Opus 5.5)	Asked Claude to draft ADR-5 about something I chose not to build.	Accepted	–	Login would need a users table and a user_id column on both expenses and budgets, which ties both domains to a third one, plus password hashing, sessions and CSRF protection. Without it the app is single-user, which is fine locally but means it must not be public as it is.
